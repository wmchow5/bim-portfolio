1. Project overview and the working method

1.1 The building and the model

  • Building: one storey on a basement. Level 1 (FFL) at +0.381 m, basement at −2.057 m, attic plate at +3.048 m. Three bedrooms, one bathroom, an open living/dining/kitchen, a laundry alcove, and a deck on the south and east with stairs to grade.
  • Systems in the model:
    • Daikin multi-split heat pump (outdoor unit CU-01 plus four wall heads)
    • Broan HRV with ducted supply and exhaust
    • domestic water with a booster pump P-01
    • sanitary and vents
    • NFPA 13D sprinklers fed from the domestic service
    • a 120/240 V panel P1 with lighting, receptacles and smoke/CO alarms
  • Model health at the end: 50/50 items closed and two known information warnings: "Domestic Tempered Water 1 cannot calculate flow" (no shower-head family) and "network needs an output element" on the range hood (placeholder hood).

1.2 The method used for every item

  1. Plan first. A floor plan, section or comparison table, with options and a recommendation, before any model change ("provide the floor plan first").
  2. Check the law before changing coursework values. If a value came from the BCIT coursework, keep it unless it breaks code. Record better options as recommendations.
  3. You choose the option.
  4. Back up the .rvt (_backup_YYYY-MM-DD_before-XX.rvt).
  5. Make the change, then check the connections (no open connectors), run a clash check and check the warnings.
  6. Save, then update both tracker copies (the Stage 1 copy, then copy it to the original).

7. Placement rules and working principles you taught

Rule 1: wall-mounted devices must be on a real wall (25 Sep)

From your correction "Where S5a is going to place? There are no wall." Check every receptacle, switch, GFCI, dedicated outlet, panel, disconnect and wall light:

  • on an actual wall face in the model, on the side of the room it serves, never in open space or a room opening
  • not inside a door opening or jamb, and not in the door swing or behind an open door
  • switches on the latch side; flag it if the latch side has no wall
  • not inside an enclosed space with no door
  • at least 100 mm of wall from any door jamb or wall end

Check method: read the walls and doors from the model (not room boxes), and trace the real walls before drawing.

Rule 2: keep switches and receptacles clear of showers and tubs (29 Sep)

  • Keep at least 500 mm horizontally from the shower or tub edge (current CEC practice). Flag 500 mm–1 m, since some inspectors apply 1 m.
  • Check against the plumbing fixtures, not just the walls.
  • If the latch side falls inside the shower zone, offer practical options: hall side, inside past the swing, or a split (your C-06 choice).

P-trap placement ("Trap P - PVC - DWV")

  • Inlet socket vertical, opening up. The U-bend below. Outlet horizontal along the drain.
  • For 3", the outlet connector sits about 59 mm above the inlet connector. This is correct, not upside down.
  • The default −X connector is the inlet. Rotate +90° about Y through the inlet, then aim the outlet about the vertical axis. Never tilt the trap about a horizontal axis.
  • Revit won't change an existing fitting's family (error "cannot be ignored"); place a new fitting.
  • Building a trap from 90° bends gives a seal of about 180 mm, too deep.

Working principles

  • Plan first, every time: a floor plan or comparison before touching the model, and say clearly when nothing has changed yet.
  • Coursework values are fixed unless they break the law. Ask whether a value came from the coursework before proposing a change. Examples kept: DW-01 location, sprinkler flows, 100 A (until you chose 200 A), single-file model. The Daikin units were changed only because you asked (M-09).
  • Follow the law first, then practice: M-01 (2 % condensate), P-21 (8-200 vs 80 %).
  • Make the model match reality: the dryer duct connected to the dryer, the T&P valve modelled, the HRV given a receptacle, the washer draining through a standpipe.
  • Back up before every change, check after every change, update both tracker copies.
